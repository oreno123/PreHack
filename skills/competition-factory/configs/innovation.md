# Innovation & Entrepreneurship Competition Config (互联网+/挑战杯)

## Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|---------------|
| **Market Researcher** | 市场痛点验证、竞品调研、用户访谈分析 |
| **Product Designer** | 产品方案设计、MVP规划、功能优先级 |
| **Business Planner** | 商业模式设计、运营策略、盈利模式 |
| **Financial Modeler** | 财务预测、融资计划、估值 |
| **Pitch Writer** | 商业计划书撰写、路演PPT |
| **Reviewer** | 审查商业逻辑可行性、数据合理性 |

## Pipeline

```
1. Problem Identify    → 痛点挖掘、需求验证（为什么做？）
2. Market Validation   → TAM/SAM/SOM、目标用户、付费意愿
3. Solution Design     → 产品/服务方案、技术路线、MVP
4. Competitive Analysis→ 竞品对比、差异化优势、壁垒
5. Business Model      → 商业模式画布、盈利模式、单位经济
6. Go-to-Market        → 获客策略、渠道、冷启动方案
7. Team & Operations   → 团队配置、里程碑、资源需求
8. Financial Projection→ 3-5年财务预测、融资计划、估值
9. Risk & Mitigation   → 风险识别与应对
10. Business Plan      → 完整商业计划书
11. Pitch Deck         → 路演PPT（10-15页）
```

## Actor-Critic Rules
- **Market Validation**: 3 rounds (data must be real, cited)
- **Financial Projection**: 3 rounds (assumptions must be consistent)
- **Business Plan**: 2 rounds
- **Pitch Deck**: 2 rounds (clarity + impact)

## Key Frameworks
- Lean Canvas (精益画布)
- Jobs-to-be-Done
- Blue Ocean Strategy (蓝海战略)
- Unit Economics (单位经济模型)
- AARRR (海盗指标)
- TAM/SAM/SOM

## Special Notes
- Numbers must be realistic and cited (no fabricated market sizes)
- Team section is important for 互联网+ — highlight relevant experience
- Technology barriers/moat (护城河) is critical
- Social impact matters for 挑战杯
- Pitch deck should be 10-15 slides, visual-heavy, minimal text
